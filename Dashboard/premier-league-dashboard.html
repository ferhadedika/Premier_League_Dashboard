    Domain & problem statement

    This dashboard investigates Premier League team performance and match outcomes from 2020/21 to 2024/25. It looks at how underlying numbers such as expected goals, possession and shots on target relate to actual results, so analysts, coaching staff and fans can see past the scoreline.
